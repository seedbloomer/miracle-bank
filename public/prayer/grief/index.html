Prayer · Grief

When someone you love is gone

Grief doesn't follow a timetable.

You may be crying every day.

You may feel numb.

You may be angry.

You may be functioning normally and then something small brings everything back.

And sometimes you may not know what you're feeling at all.

You don't have to make your grief tidy before you bring it to God.

01 / PUTTING IT INTO WORDS

What hurts most today?

Is it missing their voice?

Something you wish you had said?

A place that suddenly feels empty?

A future you thought you would have together?

Or simply the fact that they should still be here?

You don't have to explain it to us.

Tell God what hurts.

02 / A CHRISTIAN PERSPECTIVE

What do I pray when there is nothing to fix?

Grief is not a problem you have failed to solve.

Sometimes there is no next action that makes the loss okay.

The Bible gives us words of praise and hope.

It also gives us tears, questions and lament.

“The Lord is close to the brokenhearted.”Psalm 34:18

You are allowed to miss them.

You are allowed to tell God you're angry, confused or exhausted.

You are allowed to sit quietly when you don't have words.

Prayer can simply be:

“Father, this hurts. Please stay close to me.”

You do not have to rush your grief to prove that you trust God.

03 / A SIMPLE PRAYER

Pray with me

Father,

I miss them.

You know what I have lost and what this absence feels like.

Some days I know what I'm feeling.

Other days I don't.

Please stay close to me in this grief.

Hold the memories that make me smile and the ones that make me cry.

Meet me in the places that now feel empty.

When I am angry, let me speak honestly to You.

When I have questions, let me bring them.

When I have no words, stay with me in the silence.

Give me strength for today.

Help me receive comfort from the people who love me.

And when grief comes back unexpectedly, remind me that I can bring it back to You.

Father, stay close to my broken heart.

Amen.

05 / THE STEP IN FRONT OF YOU

For today

You do not need to work out how to live the rest of your life without them today.

What do you need for this day?

Rest?

Someone to sit with you?

A meal?

A conversation?

A walk?

Permission to cry?

Or simply a quiet moment with God?

Getting through today can be enough for today.

If grief is becoming overwhelming or you are struggling to cope, reaching out for human or professional support is not a failure of faith.
