Prayer · Purpose

When you don't know what you're meant to do

Maybe everyone else seems to be moving forward.

You feel stuck.

You don't know which direction to take.

Or perhaps life simply hasn't turned out the way you thought it would.

Questions about purpose can become enormous:

“What am I supposed to do with my life?”

You may not need the whole answer today.

Start with what God has already placed in front of you.

01 / PUTTING IT INTO WORDS

Where do you feel most stuck?

Work?

Family?

A decision?

A dream that hasn't happened?

A season of life you didn't expect?

Or simply the feeling that there should be something more?

Tell God where you feel lost.

02 / A CHRISTIAN PERSPECTIVE

Purpose isn't always a grand assignment

We can become so focused on discovering “God's big plan” that we overlook the people, responsibilities, gifts and opportunities already in front of us.

“Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord.”Colossians 3:23

Purpose is not only found in platforms, careers or impressive achievements.

Faithfulness matters in ordinary places too.

Caring for someone.

Doing honest work.

Learning.

Serving.

Creating.

Keeping a promise.

Showing up for someone who needs you.

You can ask God for direction without demanding that He show you the next twenty years.

God can redirect a life. Often He begins with the people, responsibilities and open doors already in front of you.

Sometimes direction begins with being faithful where you already are.

03 / A SIMPLE PRAYER

Pray with me

Father,

I don't know exactly where I'm going.

Sometimes I feel behind, stuck or unsure whether I'm using my life well.

Please guide me.

Show me what You have already placed in front of me.

Help me recognise the people I can love, the responsibilities I should carry, the gifts I should develop and the opportunities I should explore.

Close the doors that would pull me away from You.

Give me courage to walk through the ones I should enter.

Keep me from comparing my life with everyone else's.

I don't need to see the whole road.

Father, show me how to be faithful with the part I can see. Then lead me into what comes next.

Amen.

05 / THE STEP IN FRONT OF YOU

What is already in front of you?

Who needs you?

You do not need your entire life mapped out. Ask God for the next faithful step, then take it.
