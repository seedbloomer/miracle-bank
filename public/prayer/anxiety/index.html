Prayer · Anxiety

When your mind
won't stop worrying

Maybe your mind keeps jumping ahead.

“What if something goes wrong?”

“What if this gets worse?”

“What if I can't handle it?”

Sometimes there is a real problem in front of you.

Sometimes nothing has happened yet, but your mind has already travelled through ten different things that could happen.

And sometimes you don't even know why you feel anxious. You just know you can't seem to switch it off.

God is not asking you to pretend you're fine.

Start by telling Him what you're afraid of.

02 / FINDING CLARITY

Fear can make tomorrow feel like today

When we're anxious, something that might happen tomorrow can feel as real as something happening right now.

So before we pray, ask yourself:

What is actually happening right now?

Private reflection. Nothing you write here is submitted.

What am I afraid might happen?

Private reflection. Nothing you write here is submitted.

Those answers might be very different.

This isn't telling you that your fear is silly.

There may be something genuinely difficult ahead.

But you don't have to carry every possible version of tomorrow at the same time.

03 / BRINGING IT TO GOD

What does God ask us to do with anxiety?

Not hide it.

Not pretend to be peaceful.

Bring it to Him.

“Cast all your anxiety on him because he cares for you.”1 Peter 5:7

Because He cares for you.

We aren't handing God our anxiety as a technique for making ourselves calm.

We bring it to Someone who cares.

Tell Him what you're frightened of.

Ask for help.

Ask for wisdom if something needs to be done.

Ask for courage if something needs to be faced.

And ask Him for peace while you deal with what you cannot control.

What do you need from God right now?

Father, I'm afraid that...

Private reflection. Nothing you write here is submitted.

04 / A SIMPLE PRAYER

Pray with me

Father,

My mind is tired from worrying.

You know what I'm afraid of, including the things I haven't told anyone.

Some of what I'm facing is real.

Some of it hasn't happened, but I'm already frightened of what might come.

Help me see clearly.

Show me what needs my attention today and what I am carrying before its time.

Give me wisdom for anything I need to do.

Give me courage for anything I need to face.

And for the things I cannot control, help me place them in Your hands.

When my mind runs ahead again, remind me that I can come back to You.

I don't need to know everything that will happen next.

I need You with me in what is in front of me now.

Father, I give You what I'm afraid of.

Please give me Your peace and lead me through this.

Amen.

06 / THE STEP IN FRONT OF YOU

What needs your attention now?

Is there actually something you need to do?

A conversation you need to have?

Information you need instead of continuing to imagine the worst?

An appointment you need to make?

Something you've been avoiding?

Someone you need to ask for help?

If there is, identify one next step.

What can I do today?

Private reflection. Nothing you write here is submitted.

But maybe there is nothing you can do about it right now.

If you've done what you reasonably can, your next faithful step may not be more action.

It may simply be:

“Father, I've done what I can. I'm leaving this with You for today.”

07 / SUPPORT

When anxiety keeps coming back

Prayer and faith do not mean you should never feel anxious again.

If anxiety is persistent, overwhelming, affecting your daily life, or making it difficult to function, asking for help is not a failure of faith.

Talk to someone you trust and consider appropriate professional support.

You can seek help and keep praying.
