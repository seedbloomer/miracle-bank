Prayer · Health

When you're worried about your health

Maybe you're waiting for results.

Maybe you've received news you didn't want to hear.

Maybe you're living with pain, illness or a condition that doesn't seem to go away.

Or maybe it's someone you love who is unwell.

Health problems can make the future suddenly feel uncertain.

You don't have to pretend you're not frightened.

Bring that fear to God.

01 / PUTTING IT INTO WORDS

What are you most afraid of?

Is it what the doctor might say?

What treatment might involve?

Living with pain?

Losing something you used to be able to do?

What this could mean for the people you love?

Or simply not knowing what happens next?

You don't have to answer us.

Tell God what you're afraid of.

02 / A CHRISTIAN PERSPECTIVE

Can I ask God to heal me?

Yes.

You can ask God boldly for healing.

You don't have to soften the prayer because you're afraid of being disappointed.

“Lord my God, I called to you for help, and you healed me.”Psalm 30:2

Ask Him to heal.

Ask Him to guide the people caring for you.

Ask for wisdom about treatment and decisions.

Ask for strength for today.

And keep receiving appropriate medical care.

Faith does not require pretending that doctors, treatment or professional help are unnecessary.

We ask God for what we hope for while trusting Him with what we cannot know.

You can pray boldly without pretending you know what God's answer will be.

03 / A SIMPLE PRAYER

Pray with me

Father,

You know what is happening in my body and how frightened I feel.

You know the questions I have and the answers I wish I had.

Please heal me.

Give wisdom to the doctors and people caring for me.

Guide the decisions that need to be made.

Give me courage for appointments, tests, treatment and whatever comes next.

When my mind runs ahead, help me return to You.

Give me strength for today.

And when I cannot control what happens next, hold me close and help me trust You.

Father, I am asking for Your healing, Your wisdom and Your presence.

Please lead me through this.

Amen.

05 / THE STEP IN FRONT OF YOU

What needs your attention now?

Is there an appointment you need to make?

A question you need to ask your doctor?

Treatment or medication you need to follow?

Someone you need to tell?

Practical help you need to accept?

Or have you done what you can for today?

Take care of what is in your hands.
Keep bringing what isn't back to God.
