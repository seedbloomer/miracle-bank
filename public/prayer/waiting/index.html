Prayer · Waiting

When you've been waiting too long

Maybe you've been praying about the same thing for months.

Maybe years.

A job.

A relationship.

A child.

Healing.

An answer.

A door that still hasn't opened.

Waiting can become exhausting when you don't know whether anything is changing.

And sometimes the hardest question is simply:

“God, how much longer?”

You are allowed to ask Him that.

01 / PUTTING IT INTO WORDS

What are you waiting for?

What answer have you been hoping for?

What did you think would have happened by now?

What are you afraid it means because it hasn't?

You don't need to make the disappointment sound spiritual.

Tell God the truth.

02 / A CHRISTIAN PERSPECTIVE

What does trusting God while waiting actually mean?

Trusting God while waiting is not pretending you are not disappointed.

It is bringing the unanswered prayer to Him again, while remaining faithful with the life in front of you.

“How long, Lord? Will you forget me forever?”Psalm 13:1

The Bible does not hide the frustration of waiting.

You can ask God “How long?” and still trust Him.

Waiting does not mean God has left you here alone.

Waiting for one answer does not mean everything else has to stop.

03 / A SIMPLE PRAYER

Pray with me

Father,

You know how long I have been waiting.

You know how many times I have prayed about this.

I'm tired of not knowing.

Part of me still hopes.

Part of me is disappointed.

And part of me wonders whether anything will change.

Please hear me again.

If there is something I need to do, show me.

If there is a door I need to stop forcing, give me wisdom.

If I simply need to wait, give me strength to wait without letting this unanswered prayer consume everything else.

Help me remain faithful with the life You have given me today.

I still bring this hope to You.

Remind me that You are still with me here.

And I trust You with what I cannot yet see.

Amen.

What can still move while you wait?

Is there something you can learn?

Someone you can love?

Work you can do?

A different door worth exploring?

Or something good in your life that waiting has made difficult to notice?

Keep praying for what you are waiting for. God has not put your life on pause.
