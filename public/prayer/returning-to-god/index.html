Prayer · Returning to God

When you want to come back to God

Maybe you haven't prayed in a long time.

Maybe something happened and you became angry with God.

Maybe life got busy and faith slowly disappeared into the background.

Maybe you've done things you regret and you're wondering whether you have any right to come back.

Or perhaps you can't even explain what happened.

You just know that you miss Him.

You don't need to get yourself spiritually sorted out before you speak to God again.

You can start now.

01 / PUTTING IT INTO WORDS

What made you pull away?

Was it disappointment?

Something that happened in church?

A prayer that seemed unanswered?

Shame?

Doubt?

Busyness?

Something you did?

Or did you simply drift?

Whatever the answer is, you don't need to hide it from God.

Tell Him the truth.

02 / A CHRISTIAN PERSPECTIVE

Do I need the right words to come back?

No.

Jesus told a story about a son who walked away from his father and eventually decided to return home.

“But while he was still a long way off, his father saw him and was filled with compassion for him.”Luke 15:20

The son came home carrying the consequences of his choices.

But he came home.

If you have sinned, you can confess it.

If you're angry, you can say so.

If you're doubtful, bring the doubt.

If you barely know what you believe anymore, start there.

Coming back to God can begin with one honest prayer.

03 / A SIMPLE PRAYER

Pray with me

Father,

I've been away.

You know why, even when I don't completely understand it myself.

You know what I've done, what has hurt me, what I doubt and what I regret.

I don't want to hide from You anymore.

Forgive me for where I have sinned.

Help me bring You the questions I still have.

Teach me to pray again.

Help me know You, not simply know things about You.

Lead me back into Your Word and into healthy Christian community.

I don't have everything figured out.

But I'm here.

Father, I want to come back to You.

Please lead me from here.

Amen.

05 / THE STEP IN FRONT OF YOU

Start small

You don't need to rebuild your entire spiritual life tonight.

Pray again tomorrow.

Read a small part of one of the Gospels.

If you've been away from church, consider reconnecting with a healthy Christian community.

Speak to a Christian you trust.

And when you stumble again, don't make distance from God your response.

Come back.
Again and again.
