Prayer · Provision

When money is
weighing on your mind

Maybe there isn't enough for the bills.

Maybe work has slowed down, debt is building, or an unexpected expense has knocked everything sideways.

Maybe you made a financial decision you regret. Maybe you trusted someone and lost money.

Or perhaps nothing has happened yet, but you can't stop thinking:

“What if there isn't enough?”

Fear can make everything feel like one enormous problem.

Before you work out what to do next, bring the real problem to God.

01 / PUTTING IT INTO WORDS

What are you going through?

Write it as simply and honestly as you can.

Private reflection. Nothing you write here is submitted.

What are you most worried will happen?

Private reflection. Nothing you write here is submitted.

Sometimes the problem becomes clearer when we finally put the fear into words.

02 / ASKING GOD

What does it mean to ask God to provide?

God is not an ATM.

But neither does prayer mean pretending that money doesn't matter.

Jesus taught us to pray:

“Give us today our daily bread.”Matthew 6:11

You are allowed to ask God for what you genuinely need.

Ask Him.

For the money you need.

For work.

For an opportunity.

For help.

For wisdom.

For a way forward you cannot yet see.

God's provision doesn't always arrive in the way we expect.

It may come through income, work, another person, an opportunity, available help, a change you need to make, or wisdom to see something differently.

You don't have to know how God will answer before you ask Him.

03 / YOUR NEED

What do you need from God right now?

Father, I need...

Private reflection. Nothing you write here is submitted.

Now bring that need to Him.

04 / A SIMPLE PRAYER

Pray with me

Father,

You know what I'm facing.

You know what I need, what I'm afraid of, and what I wish I had done differently.

Please provide what I need.

Give me wisdom to see clearly when fear is clouding my thinking.

Show me the help I should ask for, the doors I should knock on, and anything I need to change.

If I've made mistakes, give me courage to face them and wisdom to put right what I can.

Keep fear and shame from making my decisions for me.

Help me recognise Your provision, even when it comes in a way I wasn't expecting.

Give me courage for what I can do and peace about what I cannot control.

I don't know yet how this will work out.

But I don't want to carry it without You.

Father, I bring this need to You. Please lead me from here.

Amen.

06 / THE STEP IN FRONT OF YOU

What can you do now?

Prayer is not the end of the conversation.

Sometimes the next answer is something you need to do.

Is there a bill you need to face?

A call you need to make?

Someone you need to ask for help?

Work you need to pursue?

A mistake you need to admit?

Professional advice you need to seek?

Something in your spending that needs to change?

Or have you already done what you reasonably can, and now you need to wait?

Don't try to find ten steps.

What is your next faithful step?

Today, I will...

Private reflection. Nothing you write here is submitted.

Pray first. Then take the step you can take.
