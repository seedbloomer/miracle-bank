When a relationship is hurting

Maybe someone you love has hurt you.

Maybe you hurt them.

Maybe you're barely speaking.

Maybe you keep having the same argument.

Or perhaps you're trying desperately to hold together a relationship that you cannot repair by yourself.

Before you decide what happens next, bring the relationship to God.

01 / PUTTING IT INTO WORDS

What is really hurting?

Is it what they said?

What they did?

What you did?

Something that keeps happening?

Feeling rejected, ignored or misunderstood?

Or the fear of losing the relationship altogether?

Be honest with yourself.

Then be honest with God.

02 / A CHRISTIAN PERSPECTIVE

What belongs to you?

You cannot control another person's heart, choices or response.

But you can ask God to show you your part clearly.

Is there something you need to apologise for?

Something you need to say honestly?

Someone you need to forgive?

A conversation you need to have?

A boundary you need to establish?

Or something you need to stop trying to control?

“If it is possible, as far as it depends on you, live at peace with everyone.”Romans 12:18

Notice those words:

“As far as it depends on you.”

Peace does not mean pretending nothing happened.

Forgiveness does not always mean restoring the relationship to exactly what it was.

And reconciliation cannot be forced by one person.

Ask God to show you what faithfulness looks like on your side of the relationship.

03 / A SIMPLE PRAYER

Pray with me

Father,

You know this relationship completely.

You know what happened, what was said, what was meant and what still hurts.

Please show me my own heart clearly.

If I was wrong, give me humility to admit it.

If I need to apologise, give me courage.

If I need to forgive, help me begin.

If I need to speak honestly or establish a healthy boundary, give me wisdom.

Keep anger, fear and pride from deciding what I do next.

I cannot control another person's heart.

So help me be faithful with mine.

Father, bring healing where healing is possible and give me wisdom for what comes next.

Amen.

05 / THE STEP IN FRONT OF YOU

What is yours to do?

Not what should they do.

What is yours?

Apologise?

Listen?

Speak honestly?

Forgive?

Set a boundary?

Ask for help?

Wait before responding?

You cannot control their next step.
Ask God to help you take yours faithfully.
