This week’s prayer

When money is
frightening you.

Money worries can make every decision feel urgent. You do not have to pretend you are not worried. Bring the fear to God, then take the next honest step in front of you.

A simple prayer

Father, I am worried about money.

Please provide what I need. Give me wisdom for the next step, courage to face what is in front of me, and help me trust You without pretending this is easy.

Amen.
